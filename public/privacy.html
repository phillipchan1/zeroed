Zeroed Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Zeroed is made by Made for Greatness LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use Zeroed at zeroed.madeforgreatnesslabs.com and in the Zeroed app, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account info. Your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and profile picture from Google. We only ask Google for your basic profile and email, nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you put into Zeroed. Your training plans, workouts, logs, goals, and messages you send to your coach inside the app.
  • Basic technical data. Sign-in session info and standard logs (like device type and errors) we need to keep the service working and secure.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To run Zeroed: build and adjust your training and show you your history.
  • To send sign-in codes and important service emails.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers to run Zeroed, such as cloud hosting and email delivery (Cloudflare), our database host (Neon), and AI model providers that power coaching features. They process data only to provide their services to us.

If you connect Zeroed to another app you use, such as an AI assistant, you choose what to share, and that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time through the app, and we'll do it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something.

Security

We use encryption in transit and access controls to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep yours safe.

Children

Zeroed is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For big changes, we'll let you know in the app or by email.

Contact

Made for Greatness LLC, Pasadena, CA.
